Take an instruction apart

This is the real text inside the Lesson Plan Architect, in seven parts. Press any part, and the note underneath says what that part prevents.

Seven parts, and the failure each one stops every Gem you write needs all seven
It stops a general assistant answer.

Name the job, the institution and the frameworks. “Working to Bloom and the DOK ladder” is what makes it write measurable objectives without being asked every time.

Without it: a polite, generic outline.

It stops “I cannot create documents”.

The model refuses because it thinks you want a file it must save. Tell it the truth: a lesson plan is HTML, HTML is text, and typing text is all it ever does.

Without it: the single most common Gem failure.

It stops the four-round interrogation.

Every question it will ever need, in one numbered block, with a default in square brackets so a teacher can reply “defaults” and get a plan. Ten is the ceiling.

Without it: teachers abandon the conversation.

It stops your school's template being ignored.

A precedence order settles every argument in advance: what an attached model may override, and the short list it may never override.

Without it: your six objectives become three, or three become six.

It stops invented textbook questions.

The rule that matters most for anything a learner will see. Copy word for word, keep the printed numbering, invent nothing, and stop and ask when a page is missing.

Without it: a plausible exercise 5d that is not in the book.

It stops a different-looking plan every week.

The output contract: the exact style sheet and script, so every plan prints identically. The html2pdf ban is here because the alternative garbles Arabic.

Without it: an unstyled page you cannot print.

It makes the Gem mark its own work.

Every rule above, restated as something to verify before sending. The last line, “do not print this checklist”, is what keeps it out of the answer.

Without it: the reflection arrives already filled in.

Read it as seven jobs, not as prose. Every part exists because something went wrong once, and the sentence is there so it cannot go wrong again.

Where your Gems live.

Open Gemini, find Gems in the left panel. Everything you build appears in this list, and you open one the way you would open a chat.

One empty form, three fields.

Name, Instructions, Knowledge. That is the whole product. Everything that makes a Gem good happens inside the Instructions box.

The name is how you will find it in a hurry.

Say what it makes, not what it is. “Lesson Plan Architect” beats “My Assistant” when you have nine Gems and two minutes before a class.

This one box is the entire Gem.

Paste everything between COPY FROM HERE and COPY TO HERE. Then scroll to the bottom of the box and confirm the last line arrived. A cut-off paste looks perfectly normal from the top.

This is where the 31,000-character limit bites.

On a free account there is no + button here.

Attaching documents is the one thing on this screen a free account does not get. The instruction box itself is the same size on every plan, which is why every Gem in this session is written to need no attached files at all.

Test it before you save, with the real sentence.

Type what a teacher would type. If the ten questions do not appear at once, the instructions did not arrive whole. Go back to the box and scroll to the end.

Save, then share the link with your department.

A saved Gem sits in your list for good. Share copies the Gem's own link, so anyone who opens it gets your instructions, not a copy they can break.

Three limits to measure before you design

Settle three things before you design a Gem: how long the instructions may be, what a paid plan would actually change, and where the answer is allowed to land. Each one broke a Gem that was correct in every other way.

The rule to teach: write to 28,000, not to 31,000.

                    How long the instructions can be
                    About 31,000 characters
                    About 31,000 characters. The same.
                    Paying does not buy a longer instruction block. A 42,000-character Gem is cut off on Ultra exactly as it is on a free account.
                  

                  

                    Which model answers you
                    Gemini 3 Flash-Lite, Flash and Pro
                    The same three models
                    Your Gem is not cleverer on a paid plan. The quality comes from your instructions, not from the subscription.
                  

                  

                    How much one conversation can hold
                    About 50 pages of text
                    About 200 pages on Plus, and far more on Pro and Ultra
                    This is the real difference. Your instructions, the textbook page you attach and the file it writes all share that space, which is why a long plan is cut off sooner on a free account.
                  

                  

                    How many messages before you wait
                    A standard allowance, refreshing every 5 hours
                    Twice that on Plus, four times on Pro, more on Ultra
                    On a free account, plan the building session. Testing a Gem takes many messages, and you can run out in the middle of a workshop.
                  

                  

                    Attaching documents to the Gem
                    No + button on the account we tested
                    Available
                    Write the Gem so it never needs an attached file. Both of ours are self-contained for exactly this reason.
                  

                  

                    Long video and audio uploads
                    5 minutes of video, 10 minutes of audio
                    Up to an hour of video, up to three hours of audio
                    Nothing to do with a lesson-plan or worksheet Gem. It matters only if you feed it recordings.

              Plain text = paying does change it, and the last column tells you whether that matters to you.

So what do you tell a teacher who asks whether to pay?

            
Build it free first. The Gem, the instruction box and the models are identical. Pay only when you hit one of two walls.

Paste, check, refine

          
Every Gem misbehaves in one of nine ways. Press the symptom you are seeing and take the sentence. It is written to be sent to the Gem as it stands.


          

            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
          


          

            

              
Cause: the anti-refusal paragraph did not arrive, or was never written.

              
Send this

              
A lesson plan is HTML source code, and HTML is plain text you type out. Nothing here needs a tool, a file system or an upload. Type the file now.

            

            

              
Cause: your instructions do not say “ask ONCE, never two lists”.

              
Send this

              
Put every question you still need into one numbered block now, with a default in square brackets for each. Do not ask me a second list.

            

            

              
Cause: it used Canvas, which turns the code into ordinary text.

              
Send this

              
Send the whole file again as one fenced html code block in the chat. Do not use Canvas or a document panel.

            

            

              
Cause: the output limit, not your instructions.

              
Send this

              
continue

              
It should resume from the exact character it stopped at. If it starts again from the top, say: resume from where you stopped, do not repeat anything.

            

            

              
Cause: no FORMAT AUTHORITY block, so its default outranked your attachment.

              
Send this

              
The attached model plan outranks your default. Use its number of objectives, its stage names and its order exactly. Mirror its shape back to me first, then build.

            

            

              
Cause: the interview never asked for them.

              
Add this as question 1, then rebuild

              
1. Your name, and your school's name. — Print both in the meta table. Never leave them blank and never invent them.

            

            

              
Cause: a missing checklist line.

              
Send this

              
Reflection and Next Steps must be empty — ruled lines only. The lesson has not happened yet. Rebuild the file with those two sections blank.

            

            

              
Cause: a Latin word inside Arabic text with no isolation.

              
Send this

              
Wrap every Latin word inside Arabic text in <span class="lat">…</span> so nothing glues to it. Never put English and Arabic on one line — stack them.

            

            

              
Cause, nine times out of ten: the paste was cut at 31,000 characters.

              
Do this, before anything else

              
Open the Gem, scroll to the very bottom of the Instructions box, and check the last line is “----- COPY TO HERE -----”. If it is not, shorten the block and paste it again.
