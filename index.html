What you hand in at the end of the level

One lesson of your own, carried all the way through. Every artefact below traces back to the same lesson objective you choose in Session 1.

An interactive app

A single index.html quiz or activity on your lesson topic, with instant feedback, a timer, a results screen and a completion certificate. It opens offline, on a phone.

A published teacher website

A public page with your profile, your courses and your resources, at a link you can put on a CV or send to a parent.

Your own AI tool

One custom Gem or GPT that does a task you repeat every week, written once and shared with your colleagues.

An interactive digital book

A flipbook of your lesson with audio, ready to send to a learner as a link or download for offline reading.

A reusable prompt library

The master prompts from all five sessions, with your own subject, level and colours already filled in.

The vocabulary to lead it

The ability to explain HTML, CSS and JavaScript to a room of teachers in three minutes, and to say exactly which layer a fault is in.

Every tool link in one place

Open these in a separate tab and keep them open while you work through the level. Prices, plans and interfaces change — check the current settings in your own account before you train or publish.
