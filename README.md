# Level 3 — AI Course for Teachers

**Build a real website, and understand every part of it.**

An interactive, bilingual (English / العربية) course for teachers at Monglish International Academy and Edulixa. Every page is a single HTML file that opens by double-clicking. No installation, no accounts, and — apart from the links out to other tools — no internet needed.

Created by **Dr. Abir Ahmed Aly Wafa**, Head of EdTech & Instructional Design.

## Session 2 at a glance

Nine steps. The centre of it is that a Gem is not a smarter model — it is the same model holding your instructions permanently.

- The same request sent to a plain chat and to a Gem, both answers on screen at once
- A real working instruction block taken apart into its seven jobs, each one pressable
- The Gemini Gem screen, with a note on every control and where it goes wrong
- **The three limits, measured rather than described:** the Instructions box silently cuts off near **31,000 characters**, a full free-versus-paid comparison table, and Canvas turning a runnable file into a document
- **What a paid subscription actually changes** — a seven-row comparison table. The instruction box, the models and Gems themselves are **identical on free and paid**; what changes is how much one conversation holds, how many messages you get, and whether you can attach documents. The rule to teach: **write to 28,000 characters, not 31,000**
- A live character meter: paste a block in, and it shows you the exact text that would never reach the Gem
- **The Gem Instruction Builder** — fields in, a complete paste-ready block out, with seven lamps for the seven parts
- Both finished Gems in full: **Lesson Plan Architect** and **Worksheet Architect**, with a live preview of what each produces
- Nine symptoms, each with the exact sentence to send back to the Gem
- Publishing: the Gem's link for your department, and the file it made for your learners

### The two Gems

Both are self-contained, so they work on a free Gemini account with no knowledge files, and both fit under the paste limit.

| Gem | What it makes | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **Lesson Plan Architect** | Any subject, any grade. Six measurable objectives, eight stages, three-tier differentiation in every row, a 16-mark rubric, and a printable HTML file. | 16,584 characters |
| **Worksheet Architect** | Any subject, any stage. Sections that climb the DOK ladder, marks that total, and an answer key that cannot reach the student's print. The same file is both a paper worksheet and a self-marking one. | 27,717 characters |

## The Site Builder

Open `apps/app-site-builder.html`. It asks one question, then does the rest.

1. **Your idea** — one sentence, in your own words.
2. **The draft** — eight decisions, each one telling you *why* it was made, and each one changeable.
3. **Look and feel** — twelve colour palettes, five visual styles, three navigation bars, six button styles, four galleries. Every choice is drawn as the real thing, and hovering it repaints the live preview beside you.
4. **Words and pictures** — your four pages (Home, About, Services and Courses, Contact), your header picture, your social links. Paste a picture link and it arrives on its own.
5. **Build it** — two ways out, and you can take both:
   - **Download the finished website.** One HTML file with your colours, your words and every picture embedded inside it. It opens by double-clicking and works offline.
   - **Copy the build order.** The written specification of every choice, to hand to Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT when you want something the tool cannot do.

It works in English and Arabic, mirrors right-to-left, remembers your answers on your own computer, and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## For teachers running this course

- Every page works offline. Put the folder on a USB stick if the room has no wifi.
- Both languages are in the same file — the button in the top bar switches them.
- The Site Builder saves to the browser on that computer only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Before a teacher publishes a site or anything a Gem wrote: no learner's full name, no marks, and no photograph without written permission.
- Every AI-generated item is reviewed, corrected and approved by the teacher before it reaches a learner.
